## End to end

1. `get_cpf`, then `view_tree` to open the states and maps with the largest `work=`. Read the `nestforge` skill
   page before your first move.
2. `finalize` the program as it is and `score` its library: a correct version is recorded before you change
   anything.
3. `list_move_kinds` once, then `list_moves` and `apply_move`. Each `apply_move` answers with the whole-program time;
   keep a move when it drops, else apply its inverse. `run_defaults stage=moves` applies the default fusion. After
   each move you keep, `finalize` and `score` the library.
