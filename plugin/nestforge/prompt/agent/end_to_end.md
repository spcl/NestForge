## End to end

1. `get_cpf`, then `view_tree` to open the states and doalls with the largest `work=`. Read the `nestforge` skill
   page before your first change.
2. `score` the program as it is, to know where it stands before you change anything.
3. Work your phases of the workflow in its order. `get_performance` compares your changes; `score` when a phase is
   done, to read the grade of what you keep.
